---
name: who-wins-this-work
description: Use when the user asks who usually wins a kind of government contract, what similar contracts were worth, or wants to size up competitors before bidding.
---

1. Call `search_awards` with keywords for the work (and UNSPSC prefixes if known). It covers federal (CanadaBuys) awards from the last two fiscal years.
2. Summarise: top suppliers by number and total value of awards, typical award value, the departments that buy it, and the most recent awards with links.
3. Say plainly when values aren't published or the sample is small; don't extrapolate beyond federal data.
4. If the user is looking at a specific open tender, compare it with these awards (value range, likely competitors).
